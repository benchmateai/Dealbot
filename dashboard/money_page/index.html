Wherever a venue sells a side for less than that, by more than its fee and the cost of the money until the market settles, the test makes a paper buy at the displayed price, at most $100 a market. The year-end ranges are priced straight from the options; the "hits a price" markets from a model, so trust those prices less. They settle on Jan 1. No orders are placed.
